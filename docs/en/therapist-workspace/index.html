The working day should not arrive through messages and notes

When updates are passed verbally or through group messages, small changes are easily missed. The therapist workspace brings the schedule, activity context and status together while separating required information from information the role may not access.

Does every therapist have a separate login?

A dedicated therapist login exists. Users and access are configured by the business within the approved product scope.

Can a therapist see every client?

Not necessarily. Access should be limited to the information, locations and activity required for the role.

Can it be used from a phone?

Phone use and available actions depend on the product environment, device and configured permissions.
