Before moving forward

The questions that really affect pricing.

Why is there no single price for every business?

A small spa, a hospitality property and a clinic network use the same foundation at different depths. Pricing depends on usage, locations, tools and selected integrations.

What is considered a one-time cost?

Setup, data migration or cleanup, integrations, training and special configuration are shown separately when required.

Can we start small and add more later?

Yes. The goal is to choose the right starting point and add capabilities as needed, without replacing the system or migrating again.

How do we get an exact price?

A short fit call maps the operation, selects a plan and separates recurring service from setup. A clear proposal is then prepared for approval.
