Turn a gift card from a document into a managed product

When gift cards are sold in one channel and redeemed in another, validity, balance or ownership can be lost. A connected flow shows what was sold, to whom, its status and what may be redeemed.

Can cards be sold by value or treatment?

Different product types can be assessed around the business sales model and policy.

Can buyer and recipient be different?

The journey is designed to keep the transaction, purchaser, recipient and redemption connected.

What about partial redemption?

Partial redemption and balances require clear product and usage rules before activation.

Can products appear on vouchers.co.il?

Approved products can be assessed for listing on the Vouchers marketplace. Catalog, payment and data transfer are activated only after the relevant channel fit and approval are confirmed.
