Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about fit, migration, pricing, integrations and implementation, with a distinction between what can be assessed and what is approved only after discovery.

Better answers start with the right questions

What to clarify before proposal and implementation.

Ask another question
Who is BIZonline designed for?

The new site presents solutions for spa and wellness, clinics, hospitality and lodging, and venues and events. Fit is determined by the operating workflow, not only by the industry label.

How do we know whether the platform fits?

Start by mapping services, schedules, teams, resources, payments and existing data. After a focused demo, confirm in writing which needs are covered and what requires more review or work.

Can data be migrated from another platform?

Review what can be exported, the available fields, data quality, duplicates and missing values. Migration scope and validation are defined only after a representative sample is checked.

Which integrations are available?

Every integration is assessed against the system, version, interface and permissions available. It is not presented as available or included in a proposal until it can be verified.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing separates recurring service from setup, data migration and special work. The final amount depends on approved operating scope and appears only in the current commercial proposal.

How long does implementation take?

There is no single timeline for every business. After data, configuration, integrations and team scope are mapped, a plan is created with stages, responsibilities and acceptance checks.

What is reviewed for security and privacy?

Before production, confirm permissions, data types, retention and deletion, backups, providers, forms and legal obligations. A security or certification claim is published only after verification.

How does support work after launch?

Support channels, operating hours, response targets and each side’s responsibilities should appear in the proposal or service agreement. Do not rely on an unapproved promise.
